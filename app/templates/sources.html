Methodology Note

This tool implements simplified versions of the calculation frameworks from the papers above. The formulas are calibrated to Austrian conditions using local solar data, Austrian crop statistics, and EU electricity market data.

Key assumptions: panel coverage ratio 15% (half-density standard from Fraunhofer ISE), panel efficiency η = 20%, performance ratio PR = 0.80, system CAPEX = €650/kWp, discount rate 5%, system lifetime 25 years. All values can be updated as better Austrian-specific data becomes available.

Results are estimates for planning purposes only. For investment decisions, consult a certified energy planner and agricultural advisor.
